For your nineteenth

Nineteen is a doorway. Behind you are all the years that made you. Ahead of you is a whole one — and it already has your name on it.

I hope it is full of new adventures: the kind you will tell badly, and often. I hope it is full of good friends — the ones who show up, who laugh at exactly the right moment, who make an ordinary Tuesday feel like something worth keeping.

And I hope you enjoy it. Not just the highlight reel; the whole slow thing. Take up space this year. Try the idea you have been circling. Say yes a little more often than feels comfortable.

Happy nineteenth, Jason.
